LinkedIn Ads: How I rebuilt a LinkedIn Ads strategy to break into the U.S. market

My role · Performance Marketer (Paid Acquisition)

Marketing StrategyProject ManagementCross-Functional Team ManagementPerformance marketingPaid Social Management
01Context

I was responsible for increasing visibility and demand in the U.S. market for a B2B SaaS product that already had traction in several European and MEA markets. While Google search showed existing demand, LinkedIn performance in the U.S. was inconsistent and underperforming, especially for direct lead generation.

02Problem

LinkedIn campaigns focused on lead generation were not performing well. Classic promotional ads, direct CTAs, and even message ads failed to generate meaningful engagement or quality leads.

At the same time, brand awareness in the U.S. market was low, which made bottom-funnel tactics ineffective.

03Goals
  • Define a precise target audience for an ABM approach, since the seniority approach was too wide (in coordination with Sales & Product Marketing)
  • Understand what type of LinkedIn campaigns resonate with a U.S. B2B audience in this industry
  • Improve engagement and visibility in the U.S. market
  • Build a scalable funnel that supports demand generation, not just lead capture
04What I did

I tested multiple LinkedIn ad formats and objectives to identify what worked and what didn’t:

  • Launched lead generation campaigns using single image promotional ads
  • Tested website conversion campaigns using a multi-step assessment tool & quizzes
  • Experimented with LinkedIn message ads with multiple industry leaders (which is often challenging when targeting CEO, CXO seniority since their accounts are very often managed by a third party)

After reviewing performance, it became clear that:

  • ✕Promotional messaging was not resonating
  • ✕Long conversion paths created too much friction
  • ✕Direct lead capture was ineffective without prior brand awareness
✓What worked
  • Defined ICP & funnel stages → targeted the right audience efficiently
  • Shifted to thought leadership ads → drove highest engagement
  • Engagement-first campaigns → built brand awareness and credibility in the U.S. market
  • Leveraged sales & product insights → tailored messaging to audience maturity
!What didn’t work
  • Single-image lead gen ads → low CTR, too promotional
  • 5-step assessment tool → too long, caused drop-offs
  • Messaging campaigns too salesy → low engagement
  • Any type of website conversion objective → underperformed with U.S. audience (at this stage)

Based on these insights, I shifted the strategy entirely. I paused lead generation campaigns and rebuilt the funnel with a top-down approach.

I worked closely with sales and product marketing to better understand:

  • Audience maturity in the U.S. market
  • Who was already aware of the product versus who needed education
  • Which segments required nurturing before any conversion-focused messaging

I then designed a full-funnel LinkedIn strategy with a strong focus on:

  • Engagement and branding campaigns at the top of the funnel
  • Thought leadership content instead of classic promotional messaging
  • Promoting pre and post events, which were the backbone of introducing the products to the market
  • Ads centered on expertise, insights, references, and value-driven narratives
  • Retargeting methods that allowed funnel growth to continue based on event, page and article views
05Results
~35%higher CTR compared to previous LinkedIn campaigns
Lower CPCacross engagement and traffic campaigns
Growthin post engagement and page followers
Better leadshigher-quality inbound interest once lead campaigns were reintroduced

Improved brand visibility, with audiences actively engaging with and recognizing the brand. Most importantly, LinkedIn shifted from a low-performing lead channel to a meaningful awareness and demand driver in the U.S. market.

06What I learned
  • U.S. B2B audiences engage far more with thought leadership than direct promotion
  • LinkedIn performs best when used to build trust before asking for conversion
  • Funnel maturity matters. Lead generation only works when awareness already exists
  • Aligning marketing strategy with sales and product insights is critical for success
