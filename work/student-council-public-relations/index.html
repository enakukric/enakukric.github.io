Public relations for a national student council

My role · Head of Office for Public Relations (2019 to 2020)

Public RelationsStakeholder ManagementEvent PlanningContent & VisualsBrand Communication
01The brief

The Croatian Student Council of Universities and University Colleges of Applied Sciences represents students at the national level. As Head of Office for Public Relations, I was responsible for how the council communicated with students, the media and institutions.

A large part of my mandate fell during the COVID-19 pandemic, so the council had to keep its public presence and its connection with students alive while most activities moved online.

02At a glance
  • Role: Head of Office for Public Relations
  • Mandate: 2019 to 2020
  • Focus: Media and institutional relations, events, campaigns, content
03Highlights
01

The 50th ceremonial session

As a member of the organising committee, I helped prepare the council’s ceremonial 50th session on 13 May 2019 at the Croatian Chamber of Economy in Zagreb. It brought together stakeholders from higher education and business, including a panel on the importance of internships in preparing for the labour market.

02

Council sessions around Croatia

I organised visits to local student councils across Croatia, bringing the national council closer to the students it represents. In June 2020, the council held a two-day session hosted by the Student Union of the Polytechnic of Šibenik.

03

Student satisfaction with online teaching

When teaching moved online almost overnight, the council surveyed students about their experience. I helped communicate the resulting digital publication: more than 50 pages of findings, based on 2,162 responses from students of universities of applied sciences.

01

Panels on international experience

Interviews with students who had taken part in Erasmus, and panels encouraging young people to look for international experience

02

“Stay home” campaigns

Pandemic campaigns raising awareness of social responsibility among students

03

Content and press

Blog posts, press materials for the media, and communication with ministries and institutions

05What I learned

Communication for an institution is about trust as much as visibility. Students, universities, ministries and businesses all listen for different things, and the same message has to work for all of them. Running campaigns during the pandemic also taught me to adapt quickly when the usual channels and events are suddenly off the table.
