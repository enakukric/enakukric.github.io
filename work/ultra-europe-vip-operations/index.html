VIP event operations at Ultra Europe

My role · VIP Event Operations Manager (2018 and 2019)

02Context

Ultra Europe is a three-day international electronic music festival in Split, Croatia, and part of the global ULTRA Worldwide network. Its VIP sections host the festival’s sponsors, partners and private guests. At the 2018 and 2019 editions, I coordinated one of these VIP sections, owning its operations from the first preparations before the festival to the final day.

03My responsibility
  • End-to-end operations of my VIP section throughout the festival
  • The experience and satisfaction of sponsors, partners and private guests
  • Coordination of a team of more than 50 people working in the section
04What I did
  • Planned and coordinated every stage of the VIP guest experience, from preparations before arrival to on-site hosting during the festival
  • Led and coordinated a team of more than 50 people from the first briefing to the close of the festival
  • Kept all parties aligned throughout the event: VIP sections, clients, partners, artists and the festival’s central organisation
  • Served as one of the key operational leads, resolving issues in real time so that operations ran smoothly and guests left satisfied
05Results
  • Delivered VIP operations across two consecutive festival editions, in 2018 and 2019
  • Coordinated a team of more than 50 people and a guest list of sponsors, partners and private guests
  • Was trusted to return for the 2019 edition after the 2018 festival
