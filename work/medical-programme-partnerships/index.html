Partnerships & client events for an international medical programme

My role · Project Manager, Atlantis Pre-Med (06/2022 to 12/2022)

01Context

Atlantis delivers international hospital shadowing programmes for pre-health students across Europe. As Project Manager for the Croatian programme, I owned delivery end to end and acted as the single point of contact between partner hospitals, local suppliers, participants and the senior team in Washington, DC and Italy.

02Goals
  • Establish and maintain strong partnerships with host healthcare institutions
  • Deliver a high-quality programme on time and within a tightly controlled budget
  • Ensure a safe, seamless and memorable experience for every participant
03What I did
